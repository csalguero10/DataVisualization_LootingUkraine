Both datasets are compared with the same ACLED events (four event types, Ukraine, 2022–2024): 232 damaged sites and 12 places of incident of objects recorded as stolen from 2022 onward. Explosions/Remote violence is the largest category around both groups. Compared with the national baseline, damaged sites show a higher share of Battles, while both groups show higher shares of Strategic developments. Violence against civilians is relatively more frequent around looting locations, but the small number of locations limits broader inference.
